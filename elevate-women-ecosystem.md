# Elevate Women: ecosystem description

Context file for working with other AI tools. Self-contained: paste it whole as background.

## What it is

Elevate Women is a global ecosystem that helps women move from "I would like to do something" to "I actually built it". It leads them to skills, projects, businesses, jobs and economic independence.

The core idea is learning by doing with technology, above all AI. Access to tools alone is not enough. Women also need knowledge, confidence, peers, role models, mentors, practical experience, opportunities, visibility, access to companies and access to capital. Elevate Women connects these pieces into one environment.

## Principles

- It is an ecosystem, not a funnel and not a set of separate events. A woman can enter at any stage and always finds the next step.
- The Global Hackathon is the first product and the proof of the model. It is not the whole ecosystem.
- Learn by doing: learn something, then apply it straight away.
- Elevate Women does not have to be a fund. It connects talent, projects and capital.
- The loop closes. A woman who grows inside the ecosystem returns as a mentor, speaker, judge, founder or partner and helps the next woman.

## Her path (the center of the map)

Learn → Connect → Build → Launch → Grow

| Step | What she does | Where it can lead |
|---|---|---|
| Learn | Gets skills by doing (AI tools, no-code, product building) | Change profession |
| Connect | Finds her people: peers, mentors, a team | Find a team or a project |
| Build | Creates something real | Find a job |
| Launch | Puts it into the world | Become a founder, get a grant |
| Grow | Scales it and earns | Raise investment, start earning from her project |

Not every participant becomes a founder. For many, the result is a job, a new profession or a place in a team.

## Who supports her along the whole path

- **Experts:** mentors, speakers, judges.
- **Companies:** sponsors, employers, authors of real challenges.
- **Investors:** angels, funds, accelerators.
- **Partners:** educational partners and community partners.

Every group creates value for the others. Companies get talent and projects. Experts and investors discover founders. Women get knowledge and opportunities.

## Products

Step products sit in the same column as the step they support.

| Product | Step | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Learning programs | Learn | Workshops, courses, bootcamps |
| Matching | Connect | Team and mentor matching |
| Hackathons | Build | Hackathons, challenges, sprints |
| Founder programs | Launch | Incubation, mentorship, support after the event |
| Capital access | Grow | Grants, investors, demo showcases |

Cross-cutting products work across the whole path:

- **Tools & services:** tools that simplify the path. Fintech, legal setup without a legal entity of her own, and tools for building a product.
- **Career & talent:** jobs, freelance, career change.
- **Profiles & credentials:** participant and project profiles, portfolio, certificates, verified achievements. Participation becomes something she carries into her career.
- **Community:** the permanent base under every step. Networking, peer support, accountability, finding collaborators and co-founders, showing projects. A woman may enter through one event and stay through the community.

## Current focus

Building now: **Community** and **Hackathons**. They are highlighted on the map.

The Elevate Women Global Hackathon 2026 is the first proof of the model. It tested education, connections, building, mentorship, showcase, recognition, partnerships and first financial support for winning projects. Everything else on the map is the next layer.

## Business model (direction)

Social impact plus a sustainable commercial model.

- **B2C:** possible paid programs, membership, bootcamps, founder programs, career programs.
- **B2B:** companies get access to talent, innovation through corporate challenges, brand, education and early-stage startups.
